Consent and Storage

Cookie Notice

This notice explains the cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar technologies used by the public iBridge website, what they do, and how you can manage them.

Last updated: March 10, 2026 Applies to the public iBridge website only

How This Site Uses Device Storage

The site uses a mix of browser storage and third-party technologies. Essential storage is used to keep the website secure, remember privacy choices, and support core functions. Optional analytics and external content stay off unless you choose them.

You can change your choices at any time with the Cookie Preferences button in the footer or by selecting the button below.

Third-Party Services Behind Consent

  • Google Maps for interactive map embeds.
  • Google Analytics if analytics consent is enabled and a production measurement ID is configured.
  • Microsoft Clarity if analytics consent is enabled and a production project ID is configured.

These services may process IP address, browser information, and device data. They remain disabled until you opt in, and analytics services stay inactive until a valid production configuration is in place.

How Long Consent Is Remembered

Your privacy choices are stored for up to 12 months so the site does not ask you on every visit. After that period, or if you clear browser data, we ask again.

Browser Controls

You can also manage cookies and storage through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies or all browser storage can affect site performance and may prevent some functions from working correctly.

Direct Marketing

The public site does not enable electronic direct marketing by default. If marketing features are introduced later, they will remain off until you have given an express opt-in where required by POPIA.

Questions

For questions about cookies, storage, or consent, contact info@ibridge.co.za or review the Privacy Notice and Compliance page.
