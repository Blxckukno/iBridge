South African Compliance

POPIA, PAIA and ECTA

This page summarises the main South African compliance controls surfaced through the public iBridge website and explains how privacy requests, access requests, and core website disclosures are handled.

Last updated: March 23, 2026 Aligned to the company PAIA manual, privacy policy, and public website processing

POPIA Commitments

  • Direct collection notices are provided through the Privacy Notice and form-level consent wording.
  • Optional analytics, marketing, and third-party map embeds stay off until consent is recorded.
  • Reasonable technical and organisational security measures are applied to website processing.
  • Cross-border transfers are restricted to situations POPIA permits.
  • If a security compromise triggers POPIA notice obligations, affected persons and regulators will be notified as required.

PAIA Access to Records

You may request access to records held by iBridge where PAIA applies. iBridge maintains a PAIA Manual prepared in terms of section 51 of PAIA, compiled on 01 September 2025. Requests should be sent to mgqibelo.gasela@ibridge.co.za or raised directly with the Information Officer on 083 254 2481, with enough detail for us to identify the record and verify the requester.

Request typeWhat to include
Access requestYour identity details, the record requested, why you need it if required by law, and contact information for the response.
Correction or deletion requestThe information you believe is inaccurate or should be removed, and why.
Urgent complaint or incidentA clear description of the issue, affected individuals, and any dates or screenshots that help verify the problem.

PAIA requests are generally handled within 30 days, with a further extension of up to 30 days where the request volume or search complexity reasonably requires it. Request fees and reproduction fees may apply in line with the PAIA regulations.

Typical Record Categories

  • Corporate and governance records, including incorporation documents, board records, policies, and legal disclosures.
  • Operational and service-delivery records, including contact-centre logs, scripts, quality records, and website communications.
  • Human resources, recruitment, training, employment equity, and skills-development records.
  • Financial, tax, statutory, payroll, and supplier-management records.
  • Client, supplier, partner, and cloud-service records, subject to confidentiality and lawful access limitations.
  • PAIA manuals, access-request logs, POPIA records, awareness-training records, and security-incident support records.

Processing Purposes and Recipients

In line with the PAIA Manual and Privacy Policy, iBridge processes personal information to deliver outsourced contact-centre and managed services, support financial and operational controls, manage recruitment and HR, provide training and cloud-enabled services, respond to enquiries, and comply with legal obligations.

Personal information may be supplied to client companies, payroll and training authorities, auditors, banks, regulators, quality assurance teams, recruitment verification agencies, and external IT or hosting service providers where the law or the service requires it.

Marketing and Electronic Communications

Electronic direct marketing is not enabled by default on the public website. Where marketing is introduced, iBridge will use an express opt-in model where POPIA requires it and will provide a clear opt-out channel.

Security and Incident Reporting

Suspected security issues, abuse, privacy incidents, or unlawful website activity can be reported to info@ibridge.co.za.

Include as much detail as possible, including the page URL, time observed, screenshots, and the device or browser used.

2025 Deliverables and 2026 Governance Roadmap

The 2025 year-end compliance report records the following completed deliverables for iBridge: PAIA reports submitted, the 2025 PAIA Manual update completed, security-breach assistance completed, POPIA/PAIA awareness training completed, and consultation meetings completed.
